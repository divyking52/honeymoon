03 — The atelier

What we actually do

Four promises we keep for every couple — written once, held through landing, and never handed to a call centre.

01

One planner, start to landing

The person who takes your first note is the person who meets the car. No desk in between, no script.

02

We sleep in the rooms we sell

If a suite faces a generator, or the sunset is a brochure lie, it never reaches you. We have the photographs from the pillow.

03

Tables held under our name

The beach dinner, the late museum hour, the vineyard that does not take walk-ins. They are promised to the atelier, not to a website.

04

A thread that answers at midnight

One conversation, from the fitting to the flight home. If a boat is late, you hear it from us before you hear it from the dock.

06 — A sample week

The long way home

Paris, then Venice, then Santorini. One route we keep building because the flights connect and the days do not feel copied.

  1. Day 1 · Paris

    Land, then go quiet

    A suite on the Left Bank. Supper that is not a tasting menu performed at you. Sleep.

  2. Day 2 · Paris

    After the museums close

    One private hour, then champagne on the river while the bridges turn their lights on.

  3. Day 3 · Venice

    The city that answers by water

    Morning flight, a piano nobile above a canal that is not a thoroughfare. You walk, or you don’t.

  4. Day 4 · Venice

    Before the day-trippers

    Gondola at eight. The islands of lace and glass in the afternoon, home before the crush.

  5. Day 5 · Santorini

    Whitewash, and the real blue

    Caldera suite. The photograph everyone takes is at six. Yours is at eight, from the terrace.

  6. Day 6 · Santorini

    Nothing after four

    A catamaran, a swim, a table on the cliff. The rest of the diary is blank on purpose.

  7. Day 7 · Departure

    Breakfast with the wind

    You leave when the car is called, not when a group is. The planner is already on the other end.
