03 — Hours

Evenings we refuse to rush

The beach dinner, the late museum hour, the vineyard that does not take walk-ins — promised to the atelier, not to a website.

Dinner on the sand

Stillness

A ritual for two

Oils chosen for the climate you are in, not a menu of twelve. Ninety minutes, then the day is yours again.

The private yacht

No other guests, a captain who knows the quiet side of the island, and lunch that is not in a box.

Air

Balloon at first light

You rise before the roads do. A basket for two, then breakfast back on the ground while it is still cool.

Glass

The glass cabin

A night under a roof you can see through. The aurora does the talking. We book the cabin that actually faces north.
